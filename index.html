Install

How to install

Porsche Electric isn’t in the App Store. Five steps, about ten minutes, only once.

Why the extra steps? The app is signed with George’s private developer certificate, not through the App Store. iOS will only install it on iPhones that George has registered by their device ID (UDID) — so your iPhone has to be added first.
1

Find your iPhone’s UDID

The UDID is a long code that identifies your iPhone. Pick whichever is easiest:

  1. Connect your iPhone to the Mac with a cable and unlock it (tap Trust if asked).
  2. Open Finder and click your iPhone in the sidebar under Locations.
  3. Click the grey line under the iPhone’s name a few times until it shows UDID.
  4. Right-click it and choose Copy UDID.
  1. Install Apple Devices (or iTunes) from the Microsoft Store.
  2. Connect your iPhone with a cable, unlock it and tap Trust.
  3. Select the iPhone; under General, click the line with the serial number until it shows the UDID.
  4. Select it and copy it with Ctrl + C.
  1. Open get.udid.io in Safari on your iPhone and tap Tap to find UDID.
  2. Allow the download, then go to Settings › Profile Downloaded and install it.
  3. Safari opens again and shows your UDID — copy it.
  4. Afterwards you can remove the profile under Settings › General › VPN & Device Management.

This uses a third-party website. If you’d rather not, use a Mac or PC.

2

Send it to George and wait for the go-ahead

Send George your UDID and your iPhone model (e.g. iPhone 17 Pro) in a message. He registers your iPhone and publishes a new build on this page, then lets you know.

Don’t install before he says it’s ready — until your iPhone is in the build, iOS refuses with “Unable to Install”.
3

Install from this page, in Safari

Open this page on your iPhone in Safari (not Chrome, and not inside WhatsApp, Instagram or Mail), tap the button and confirm with Install. Then go to your Home Screen and watch the icon load.

Install Porsche ElectricVersion 1.0 (1) · 23 Oct 2026

On a computer? Scan this with your iPhone’s camera to open the page there.

On the Home Screen it’s called Porsche Electric. Already have an older build? Just install over it — your sign-in stays.

4

Turn on Developer Mode

The first time you open the app, iOS says “Developer Mode required”. That’s expected for privately signed apps:

  1. Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode (near the very bottom).
  2. Turn it on and tap Restart.
  3. After the restart, unlock the iPhone, tap Turn On and enter your passcode.

The Developer Mode switch only appears after the app is installed. You only do this once.

5

Open it and sign in

Tap the person icon at the top right (that’s also where the colour schemes are) and sign in with your My Porsche account (Porsche ID) — the same one you use in Porsche’s own app. Your car appears in a few seconds. Unlocking asks for your S-PIN.

Just curious? Tap Use demo data instead to try every view without a car.

If something doesn’t work

“Unable to Install” or “Integrity could not be verified”

Your iPhone isn’t in the current build yet. Check that George has your UDID and has confirmed the new build, then tap Install again. If the download started before he updated the page, delete the half-installed icon first.

Nothing happens when I tap “Install Porsche Electric”

Only Safari can install apps from a web page. If you opened the link in another browser or inside a messaging app, copy the address and paste it into Safari.

I can’t find Developer Mode in Settings

It only shows up once a privately signed app is on the iPhone. Install the app first (step 3), try to open it once, then look again under Settings › Privacy & Security, at the very bottom.

“Untrusted Developer”

Go to Settings › General › VPN & Device Management, tap Apple Development: George Schell and choose Trust. Then open the app again.

The app suddenly won’t open any more

Privately signed builds have an expiry date; the current one runs until 28 February 2027. Before then, George will post a new build here — just install it again over the old one. Your sign-in and data stay.

I have an older version installed — do I need to delete it?

No. It’s the same app with a new name: install the new build from this page and it replaces the old one (it may have been called “My Cayenne” or “Electric Porsche”), keeping your sign-in and settings.

Does it work with my car?

It’s made for the Cayenne Electric and the Macan Electric. The data comes from Porsche Connect, and the app shows a 3D Cayenne or Macan depending on what your account reports.

How do I get updates?

Come back to this page and tap Install again. The version and date on the button show which build is online.

Is my Porsche password safe?

Your password goes straight from your iPhone to Porsche’s own login server — it never passes through George or any other server. The app keeps only the login session, in the iPhone’s Keychain. For unlocking, it sends just a hash of your S-PIN, never the PIN itself.
